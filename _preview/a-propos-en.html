What writing a detection rule forces me to understand

An attacker's behaviour, not just the tool they happen to use:

  • which events it generates;
  • what separates it from legitimate activity;
  • where the threshold for raising an alert actually sits.

I like what comes next just as much: triage, investigation, cutting down false positives, and the documentation that lets someone else retrace the investigation.
