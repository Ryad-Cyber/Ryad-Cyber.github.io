Thinking like a defender

Writing a detection rule means understanding an attacker's behaviour, not just the tool being used:

  • which events and logs the activity generates;
  • how to distinguish suspicious behaviour from legitimate activity;
  • how to define a useful alert threshold.

I am also interested in what follows an alert: triage, investigation, reducing false positives and documenting findings so that another analyst can understand and reproduce the investigation.
